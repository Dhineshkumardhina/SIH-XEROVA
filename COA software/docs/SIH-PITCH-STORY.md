## 1. The Core Problem: The Siloed Railway Maintenance Paradox

Indian Railways is the 4th largest railway network in the world, carrying 24+ million passengers and 4+ million tonnes of freight every single day.

However, maintenance planning currently suffers from a structural operational challenge:

```text
  [Civil Engineering]          [Signal & Telecom]              [Traction / OHE]
    Asks for 2-hour block        Asks for 1.5-hour block        Asks for 2-hour block
          |                            |                            |
          +----------------------------+----------------------------+
                                       |
                   Result: 5.5 Hours of Cumulative Line Downtime!
                   Result: Cascading Freight Delays & Punctuality Loss!
```
